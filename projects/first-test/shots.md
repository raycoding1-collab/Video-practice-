# Rainy Morning Coffee

**Idea:** A barista's quiet first coffee of the day while rain falls outside.
**Length:** 3 shots × 5 s = 15 s · **Aspect:** 16:9 · **Tool:** Dreamina (Seedance)
**Budget:** Day 1 = shots 1–3 (check your free credit balance before starting; if it only
covers 2 clips, do shots 2 and 3 today and shot 1 tomorrow)

## Blocks (paste word for word)

**Character:** a woman in her late 20s with a short black bob haircut and round gold
wire-rimmed glasses, wearing a rust-orange chunky knit sweater with the sleeves pushed up
and a dark green canvas apron

**Location:** a small corner coffee shop with a large rain-streaked front window, a worn
oak counter, a polished brass espresso machine, hanging green plants and warm Edison bulbs

**Style:** cinematic 35mm film look, soft warm morning light, shallow depth of field,
gentle film grain, muted teal and amber colour palette, photorealistic

## Step 0 – Character reference (image only)

> Waist-up portrait of the barista standing behind the counter, looking at the camera with
> a calm half-smile, plain neutral pose. A woman in her late 20s with a short black bob
> haircut and round gold wire-rimmed glasses, wearing a rust-orange chunky knit sweater with
> the sleeves pushed up and a dark green canvas apron. Cinematic 35mm film look, soft warm
> morning light, shallow depth of field, gentle film grain, muted teal and amber colour
> palette, photorealistic

Use the best result as the reference image for shots 2 and 3 if Dreamina offers that option.

## Day 1

### Shot 1 – Outside in the rain (5 s)

- **Purpose:** Set the mood and the place.
- **Keyframe image prompt:**
  > Wide exterior shot at dawn of a small corner coffee shop glowing warmly through its
  > large front window, light rain falling, the wet empty street reflecting the warm light.
  > A small corner coffee shop with a large rain-streaked front window, a worn oak counter,
  > a polished brass espresso machine, hanging green plants and warm Edison bulbs.
  > Cinematic 35mm film look, soft warm morning light, shallow depth of field, gentle film
  > grain, muted teal and amber colour palette, photorealistic
- **Motion prompt:**
  > Steady light rain falls and ripples in the puddles. The camera slowly pushes in toward
  > the glowing window. No people walk past.
- **Camera:** slow push-in
- **Continuity:** none (opening shot)
- **Save as:** `clips/01.mp4`

### Shot 2 – The pour (5 s)

- **Purpose:** Show the craft, close and tactile.
- **Keyframe image prompt:**
  > Close-up on the oak counter of the barista's hands pouring steamed milk from a steel
  > pitcher into a white ceramic cup, a latte-art heart half formed, steam rising, her
  > rust-orange knit sleeves pushed up at the edge of frame. A woman in her late 20s with a
  > short black bob haircut and round gold wire-rimmed glasses, wearing a rust-orange chunky
  > knit sweater with the sleeves pushed up and a dark green canvas apron. A small corner
  > coffee shop with a large rain-streaked front window, a worn oak counter, a polished brass
  > espresso machine, hanging green plants and warm Edison bulbs. Cinematic 35mm film look,
  > soft warm morning light, shallow depth of field, gentle film grain, muted teal and amber
  > colour palette, photorealistic
- **Motion prompt:**
  > Milk pours in a smooth steady stream and the latte-art heart finishes forming. Steam
  > curls upward. The camera stays still.
- **Camera:** static
- **Continuity:** sweater colour and counter wood must match Step 0.
- **Save as:** `clips/02.mp4`

### Shot 3 – First sip by the window (5 s)

- **Purpose:** The payoff: a quiet moment for herself.
- **Keyframe image prompt:**
  > Medium shot of the barista sitting at a small table beside the rain-streaked front
  > window, holding a white ceramic latte cup in both hands and looking out at the rain.
  > A woman in her late 20s with a short black bob haircut and round gold wire-rimmed glasses,
  > wearing a rust-orange chunky knit sweater with the sleeves pushed up and a dark green
  > canvas apron. A small corner coffee shop with a large rain-streaked front window, a worn
  > oak counter, a polished brass espresso machine, hanging green plants and warm Edison
  > bulbs. Cinematic 35mm film look, soft warm morning light, shallow depth of field, gentle
  > film grain, muted teal and amber colour palette, photorealistic
- **Motion prompt:**
  > She slowly lifts the cup, takes a small sip and smiles softly. Raindrops run down the
  > glass behind her. The camera slowly pushes in.
- **Camera:** slow push-in
- **Continuity:** same cup as shot 2; same face, glasses and sweater as Step 0.
- **Save as:** `clips/03.mp4`
